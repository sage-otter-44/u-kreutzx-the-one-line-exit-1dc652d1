## Entry

> I resign from the escape room: after three years searching for the exit, I finally found it, and it turns out it was this email.

One line, 128 characters. A specific job (escape room attendant), a concrete three-year search, and the punchline lands on the last word: the exit he spent three years hunting was the resignation email itself.

## How it runs

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the single-line resignation letter to `/output/entry.txt` and checks the gate first: one line, 1–140 characters.
